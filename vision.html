Our Vision & Mission

Our Commitment to the Community

Hulhumalé Hospital is deeply rooted in our community and committed to serving its health needs. We actively engage in health promotion activities, community education programs, and preventive care initiatives. We believe that a healthy community is built through partnership, and we work closely with local organizations, schools, and government agencies to promote wellness and disease prevention.

Our commitment extends beyond treating illness to actively improving the overall health status of our community. We organize regular health screening camps, vaccination drives, and awareness programs on important health topics. We also provide support for vulnerable populations and ensure that financial constraints do not prevent anyone from receiving necessary medical care.

VISION

To enrich lives through excellence in health care services

MISSION

Our Mission is to provide quality evidence-based, people-centered health care to our customers and preventative services to our community through a team of competent staff, who undergo continuing professional development

VALUES

We keep ourselves well-educated so that we can apply new developments in our fields. We use innovative approaches to health care and customer service.

Unity

Teamwork is central to our work. We each take the responsibility to contribute effectively to teams who believe that addressing a broad range of human needs is the best way to improve a person’s health and building a strong stakeholder partnership to enrich lives.

Learning and Development

Learning signifies the acquisition of knowledge, and Development signifies the enhancement and existing skills, processes and services.

Sustainability

We believe that patients deserve to have timely access to health care and that our systems should reflect this value. People who come to the Hulhumale’ hospital can expect polite, friendly helpful staff members who relate to each person as an individual, recognizing their history, relationships, culture, and needs. We go the extra mile to meet each and every customer’s expectation.

Excellence

We believe that providing high-quality, accessible health care is our reason for being. We seek to be a model for other community health centers

To enrich lives through excellence in health care services.
